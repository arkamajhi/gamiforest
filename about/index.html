What is GamiForest?

GamiForest is an independent, curated discovery platform connecting forest games, creative prototypes, environmental simulation, forestry research and the GamiFOREST workshop series at GamiFIN.

How entries are selected

Each record should have a traceable primary source, such as a project website, a conference programme, a DOI or an institutional repository. We distinguish playable games from research prototypes and broader technology projects. A conference programme entry does not automatically imply a peer-reviewed publication.

Is this the official GamiFIN website?

No. GamiForest is an independently developed directory and archive. Official conference materials remain available at gamifinconference.com.

Suggest a correction or missing entry

We welcome documentation, corrections and missing records. Use the contribution form to prepare a source-based submission.
